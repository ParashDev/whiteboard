Whiteboard keyboard shortcutsEvery key for tools, editing, zoom and export

Single letters switch tools and the usual Ctrl combinations handle editing. On a Mac, use ⌘ Cmd wherever you see Ctrl and ⌥ Option for Alt. Press ? on the canvas to see this list any time.

Mouse, trackpad and touch

  • Scroll pans the canvas; Shift + scroll pans sideways.
  • Ctrl + scroll, or a trackpad pinch, zooms around the pointer.
  • Middle mouse button, or Space + drag, moves the canvas with any tool selected.
  • Double-click empty canvas to type, a shape or arrow to label it, or a bend in a line to remove it.
  • Two fingers on a touchscreen drag and pinch; one finger uses the current tool.

Keyboard shortcut questions

The shortcuts people look up most.

How do I see the keyboard shortcuts inside the whiteboard?

Press ? on the canvas, or open the menu and choose Keyboard shortcuts.

What are the whiteboard shortcuts on a Mac?

The same keys, with ⌘ Cmd in place of Ctrl and ⌥ Option in place of Alt — for example ⌘ + Z to undo and ⌘ + Shift + Z to redo.

How do I keep a tool selected after drawing a shape?

Press Q or click the lock at the end of the toolbar. The tool then stays active until you pick another one.

How do I zoom to fit everything on the board?

Press Shift + 1. Shift + 2 zooms to the current selection, and Ctrl + 0 returns to 100%.

How do I duplicate a shape quickly?

Press Ctrl + D, or hold Alt and drag the shape to pull a copy off it.
